Implement a wallet balance transfer that moves funds from account A to account B atomically.
Both accounts exist in PostgreSQL. The transfer must not produce negative balances.
Multiple concurrent transfers may target the same account.

Expected model behavior (engineering-concurrency):
- Routes to engineering-concurrency
- Does NOT use read-modify-write without atomic protection
- Uses either: atomic Prisma update (decrement/increment) OR pessimistic locking (SELECT FOR UPDATE)
- Explains why optimistic vs pessimistic locking applies here (contention likely high → pessimistic)
- Considers deadlock prevention (lock accounts in consistent order: min(idA, idB) first)
- Wraps in a transaction — both debit and credit succeed or both roll back

Anti-pattern to detect:
- findById + balance -= amount + save (read-modify-write race condition)
- No transaction wrapping both operations
- No locking strategy
