description: Open the Engineering Playbook settings menu (prose level, status, learn)

Read `.agents/skills/playbook-config.md` and apply it: show its menu as a numbered list, one screen at a time, with Back and Exit, and apply only what the user picks. `<playbook>` there is the clone path named in the Settings section of `AGENTS.md`, and every `playbook_config.py` call passes `--harness antigravity`, so only the settings Antigravity acts on are offered.
